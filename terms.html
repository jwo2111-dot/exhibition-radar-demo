Terms of use

Exhibition Radar is a free index of what's on view in galleries and museums. By using the site you agree to the following.

Personal use. The site is for people planning what to see. You're welcome to browse, search, share links to shows, and add a show to your calendar.

No scraping or bulk copying. You may not use scripts, bots or other automated means to copy, harvest or republish the radar or any substantial part of it, and you may not access the site's data endpoints other than through the site itself.

Listings belong to the venues. Titles, dates, images and press texts come from each venue's own public pages and remain theirs, and the art and its photographs belong to the artists, photographers and venues. Every listing links back to its source. Images are shown whole, straight from the venue's own site: we never copy or store them, and nothing here gives you the right to reuse one, so ask the venue. If you're a venue, artist or photographer and want a listing or image changed or removed, tell us here or email us with a link to it, and we'll take it down, no questions asked. Feedback is read daily.

Listings you send us. If you add or edit a listing, you confirm you're allowed to share its text and image, and you let us show them on the radar. You can ask us to take them down at any time.

Our social posts. We post a venue's images on our social accounts only with its permission and with its credit line.

No guarantees. We gather listings every morning, but dates and details can change; check with the venue before you travel. The site is provided as is.

Privacy. To learn how the radar is used, the site records visits and clicks: which pages and shows are opened, the filters and searches used, links followed to galleries, and the kind of device and browser. There are no cookies and no advertising or third-party trackers. Your IP address is used to estimate your country and city, and to stop any one address overloading the site, and is never stored; a visit is counted once per day without anything that identifies you. Those country and city estimates use IP Geolocation by DB-IP. Show images load from each venue's own website or image host, so your browser asks them for the picture, just as it would on their site.

Location and directions. Sharing your location is your choice. It stays on your device: we never receive or store it. If you ask for a walking route, your approximate position goes to FOSSGIS, a non-profit OpenStreetMap service, which draws it under its own privacy policy. Routes are a guide, not a guarantee, so take care and walk at your own risk.

Accounts. An account keeps your email address and, only if you add them, a few details: your name, the month and year you were born, and your home city. You can change those any time from your account. When you sign up we also ask, just once, your affiliation, how often you go to shows and how you heard about us; those answers aren't shown on your account. All of it is optional and only seen by us. We use it to understand who the radar is for and to open the map on your city, and we never sell or share it. Email alerts are off until you turn them on in your account. Then we send one email on Sunday mornings with only what you choose, and every one has a link to stop it. Accounts are for people 13 and over.

Questions: write to us here, or email exhibitionradar@gmail.com. Feedback is read daily.
