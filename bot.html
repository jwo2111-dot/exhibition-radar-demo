ExhibitionRadarBot

If you’ve seen ExhibitionRadarBot in your logs, that’s us. Exhibition Radar is a free index of art exhibitions. We read exhibition pages once a day so that someone looking for a show can find yours, and every listing links back to you.

To stop it

Add this to your robots.txt and we’ll skip you from the next day on:

User-agent: ExhibitionRadarBot
Disallow: /

We check it before every visit, so that’s all it takes. You don’t need to tell us, and we won’t try to talk you out of it. If something’s listed wrongly, tell us here, or email exhibitionradar@gmail.com. Feedback is read daily.

What we show

  • The facts of a show: artist, title, dates, your gallery and address, and a link to your page.
  • The opening of your press release, credited to you, with a link to read the rest on your site. Never the whole thing.
  • One image, loaded from your server. We keep no copy, so if you change or remove it, it changes or disappears here too.

How it behaves

  • Reads your robots.txt first, Crawl-delay included.
  • At least 1.5 seconds between requests, once a day, then cached for 20 hours.
  • Public exhibition pages only, never anything behind a login or a form.

The point of all this is to send people to your door.
